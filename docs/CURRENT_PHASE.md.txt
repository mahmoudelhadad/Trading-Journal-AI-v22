## Objective

Let a trade be entered by hand as the sequence of executions it actually was —
multiple entries, partial exits, and re-entry while the position remains open —
without introducing a second execution model, a migration, or any change to the
aggregate surface that every existing calculation reads. Imported NinjaTrader
trades, generic CSV/XLSX import, Forex/CFD behaviour, analytics, export, and
backup/restore remain unchanged.

---

## Delivered Scope

1. **Optional Manual Scale In / Out section.** A collapsed-by-default
   `SCALE IN / OUT (Optional)` section on the manual trade form. A trade
   entered without it takes the unchanged v1.2 path.

2. **Multiple entries and partial exits.** Several entry legs and several
   partial exit legs are supported, including a partial exit followed by a
   re-entry while the position is still open.

3. **Flat-to-flat enforcement.** A leg sequence returning the position to flat
   before the final leg is rejected, naming the time it goes flat. Over-exit
   and entry/exit quantity imbalance are also rejected.

4. **Leg-derived aggregates.** While manual legs exist they are the form's
   source of truth and derive `positionSize`, `entryPrice`, `exitPrice`,
   `entryTime`, and `exitTime`. Downstream calculations stay aggregate-driven.

5. **Exact arithmetic and one numeric grammar.** Weighted averages use bigint
   scaled-integer arithmetic. A single decimal grammar governs validation,
   decimal-place limits, parsing, folding, and derivation, so no two layers can
   disagree.

6. **Honest manual provenance.** `sourceExecutionId` became optional; manual
   legs omit it entirely rather than carrying a fabricated broker ID. Imported
   legs are read-only.

## Manual Scale Contract

- The Scale section is **optional and collapsed by default**. A trade with no
  legs behaves exactly as it did in v1.2.
- Direction is chosen once on the trade and inherited by every leg; it must be
  set before legs are added.
- Manual legs reuse the existing v1.2 `legs` array. There is no parallel manual
  execution model and no new persisted structure.
- Imported broker legs are **read-only**: the section renders read-only
  whenever a source platform is present, and the save-candidate builder returns
  imported trades untouched.
- The v1.2 NinjaTrader import contract — two required CSV files, fail-closed
  all-or-nothing reconciliation, four supported roots, composite-provenance
  idempotency — is unchanged and was not reopened.

---

## Logical Trade Contract

One Journal Trade is one **completed flat-to-flat episode**, for manual entry
exactly as for import. Multiple entries, partial exits, and re-entry all belong
to the same trade while the position remains non-zero. The trade closes when
the position returns to zero, and returning to zero before the final leg is an
error rather than a second implicit trade.

---

## Aggregate Contract

- `entryPrice` — quantity-weighted average of entry legs, exact.
- `exitPrice` — quantity-weighted average of exit legs, exact.
- `positionSize` — **total entry-side transacted quantity, not peak
  simultaneous exposure.** For `Entry 2 → Exit 1 → Entry 1 → Exit 2` it is 3,
  though exposure never exceeded 2. This is the only multiplier for which
  `size × (weighted-average exit − weighted-average entry)` equals the exact
  realized difference, and it matches what v1.2 already persists for imported
  trades.
- P/L, R, points, and outcome remain derived from the aggregates. No
  calculation, analytic, chart, export, or backup path had to change.

Weighted averages are computed in bigint scaled integers with long division and
trimmed decimal output — never `Number`. Prices retain 8 decimal places and
quantities 4, wider than the v1.2 adapter's 4, because manual entry must
express five-decimal forex prices and fractional lots.

---

## Numeric and Time Contract

- One decimal grammar for every layer of the scale path.
- Forex fractional lots accepted, including `.5`.
- Futures quantities must be whole contracts.
- Exponent notation rejected (`1e2`), as are `Infinity` and `0x1F`. `Number()`
  accepts these and the bigint layer cannot parse them; that split is what
  previously let an unparseable quantity reach the save pipeline.
- Manual leg times are entered as `HH:mm` and persist as `HH:mm:00`. The trade
  date propagates to every manual leg.
- **UI editing order is stable while typing.** Rows do not move mid-edit.
- **Canonical chronological order** governs validation, derivation, and save. A
  saved trade reopens in canonical order.

## Architecture Decision

**AD-022 — Manual Scale In/Out: Leg-Derived Aggregates and Stable Editing
Order** was recorded. It fixes that the existing `legs` array is reused rather
than paralleled, that legs are the form source of truth while downstream
calculations stay aggregate-driven, that absent `sourceExecutionId` means no
broker provenance, that UI editing order is deliberately independent of
canonical calculation order, that `positionSize` means total entry-side
transacted quantity, that imported broker legs are read-only, and that manual
weighted aggregates use exact decimal/bigint arithmetic.

---

## Migration Impact

None. Manual legs reuse the optional `legs` array introduced in v1.2, and
`sourceExecutionId` became optional, which widens the accepted set and never
narrows it. No storage key, namespace, or backup format version change —
backup remains format version 2, and existing v1.2-shaped backups still
restore. No historical data was rewritten and no trade was migrated to populate
legs.

## Known Limitations

Non-blocking. None of these is a release blocker.

P2:
- Externally malformed data carrying `sourceExecutionId` without a
  `sourcePlatform` is not specifically classified.
- There is no full DOM-level integration harness for `TradeForm.handleSave`;
  the logic is unit-covered.
- Pre-existing reliance on `window.confirm` for destructive actions: a browser
  configured to suppress dialogs can silently disable deletion.

P3:
- Narrow/mobile visual coverage of the Scale section is partial.
- The explicit equal-time A/B runtime case is partial, though unit-covered.
- Externally malformed manual leg seconds other than `:00` are tolerated rather
  than rejected.
- Removing every leg from a never-saved trade can leave the unlocked aggregate
  fields blank.
- A pre-existing `Trade not saved` message can persist until the next save.
