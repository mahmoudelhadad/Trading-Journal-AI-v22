## Objective

Ensure active deterministic calculations never invent account, direction,
instrument, numeric, or date truth; reject new invalid data before it is
persisted; and leave existing inconsistent historical data raw and unmodified,
with only the affected derived calculations becoming unavailable through the
existing null contract.

## Validation Contract

New manual, imported, and restored active records must satisfy:

- `accountId` present and resolving to an active account.
- `date` canonical `YYYY-MM-DD` and calendar-valid.
- `symbol` present and supported by the calculation table.
- `direction` exactly `Long` or `Short`; blank is permitted only for
  incomplete records carrying no direction-dependent calculation inputs.
- Optional price fields and position size, when present, finite and greater
  than zero.
- Commission, when present, finite; zero and negative are permitted.
- Times, when present, canonical `HH:mm`.

`NaN`, `Infinity`, malformed numeric strings, invalid calendar dates, and
ambiguous slash dates are rejected. Browser locale must not decide an
ambiguous import's meaning.

---

## Account Resolution Contract

When account or broker text is present:

- Matching is case-insensitive and exact against active account names.
- Exactly one match is accepted; zero and multiple are rejected.

When no account text is present:

- Exactly one active account is assigned.
- Zero and multiple active accounts are rejected.

Tombstoned accounts are never candidates. There is no substring matching, no
first-account guessing, and no `acc_1` truth fallback.

---

## Historical Orphan Contract

An active historical trade referencing no active account is preserved exactly.
It is not reassigned, deleted, quarantined, or marked in storage.

Derived behavior:

- `_capital` is null.
- `_rPct` is null.
- No running-capital bucket is created for the orphan.
- Account-independent calculations remain available when their own inputs are
  valid.

The trade row displays `⚠ Unresolved account` with the tooltip
"This trade references an account that is no longer available. Account-capital
calculations are unavailable."

---

## Calculation Contract

The synthetic $10,000 account-capital fallback is removed with no replacement
number. Formulas are unchanged; a calculation returns null when required truth
is unavailable. Direction-dependent calculations require exactly `Long` or
`Short`; instrument-dependent calculations require a supported symbol; numeric
operands must satisfy the finite and positive rules where applicable. Risk
remains direction-independent.

---

## Restore Contract

The portable v1/v2 backup format and version are unchanged. Before any save,
restore performs structural validation, RawTrade domain validation,
determination of the effective active accounts, and relationship validation.
Supplied backup accounts are used when present; the currently persisted
accounts are loaded when accounts are omitted. An active orphan supplied trade
is rejected before the first write. Tombstoned historical trades remain
preservable. No transaction or rollback redesign was made.

## Known Limitations

- Zero-active-account import rejection retains automated/source-only
  evidence.
- Historical inconsistent data is characterized, not repaired; no
  reconciliation of orphan trades back to an account is provided.
- Validation is scoped to the current domain rules and is not the generalized
  import framework deferred to a later phase.
- The multi-filter comparison label and the calendar-invalid date validator
  retain source/automated evidence as described above.
