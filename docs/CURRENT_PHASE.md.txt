## Objective

Let a simulated Replay position be built and unwound as the sequence of
executions it actually was — same-side Scale In, partial Scale Out, repeated
partial exits, Scale In after a partial exit and a final Exit — while remaining
one flat-to-flat trade with one moving cost basis, one immutable stop anchor,
one cumulative anchored risk and one final R, on the released schema-v1
persistence contract and with no migration.

## Delivered Scope

### Scaling and aggregate model

- Manual same-side Scale In on an open episode with positive safe-integer
  whole-contract quantities; opposite-side entries and reversal are rejected.
- Partial Exit / Scale Out, repeated partial exits and Scale In after a partial
  exit, never exceeding the remaining quantity and never clamped.
- Final Exit returns the episode to Flat and closes the trade.
- One `tradeId` per flat-to-flat episode, reused by every execution in that
  episode and never reopened; one unique `actionId` per execution.
- `openAggregate` projects side, entered, exited and remaining quantity, moving
  weighted-average basis, realized gross P/L, stop anchor and anchored risk.

### Arithmetic and risk

- Moving weighted-average cost basis over the remaining inventory only; exited
  contracts never re-enter the average.
- Intermediate realized gross P/L recognized at each partial exit against the
  basis as it stood immediately before that exit.
- Exact scaled arithmetic through private rational internals, converted back to
  Number only at the derived-projection boundary.
- Released IEEE-754 Number behavior preserved for structurally legacy
  one-Entry / full-Exit histories.
- Weighted final Entry and Exit aggregates for closed scaled trades, with
  points, ticks and gross P/L conserved against the sum of the partial
  realizations.
- Immutable common stop anchored by the episode's first Entry; a stopless
  episode stays stopless; partial exits never reduce the risk denominator.
- Cumulative anchored risk summed per Entry, and final R from gross P/L over
  that cumulative anchored risk.

### Replay, concurrency and UI

- Scaling-aware rewind hides future scaled actions without truncating canonical
  history, and mutations remain blocked below the action high-water mark.
- Canonical sequence ordering for executions that share one decision time.
- Stale cross-tab conflicts invalidate the captured candidate instead of
  reshaping it: no quantity clamp, no candidate rebuild, no regenerated action
  identity, no automatic retry and no recaptured fill; recovery requires
  explicit hydration and fresh user intent.
- Completed sessions remain terminal and read-only, with replay navigation
  non-persisted.
- Minimal scaling UI: Scale Quantity with same-side Scale In, Exit Quantity with
  Exit and Exit All, immutable stop display and an aggregate summary. No
  reversal control, no stop editing, no unrealized P/L.

## Preserved Contracts

`schemaVersion` remains 1 and `actionVersion` remains 1. The persisted Entry and
Exit shapes are unchanged: no `legId`, no cached aggregate, no persisted
rational or BigInt value and no persistence redesign. `openAggregate` is the
canonical B2c open projection; `openPosition` is retained only as a
compatibility projection with no B2c semantic authority.

Sessions written by released v1.8 remain readable without migration, and a
released v1.8 client handed B2c-shaped schema-v1 history fails closed on those
bytes rather than rewriting them.

Journal trades, accounts and storage remain isolated from Replay sessions. The
historical market-data domain remains a separate read-only reconstructable
cache, and the read-only broker-integration policy is unchanged. Unchanged:
Journal IndexedDB schema and LocalStorage contracts; Backup, Restore and
Recovery Bin behavior; account/trade models; Supabase and sync; historical
market-data schema and mutation contracts; and B2a Replay reveal, chart, import
and visibility behavior.

B2c does not include Journal promotion or trade provenance promotion into the
Journal; cloud or multi-device Backtest session sync; broker execution or order
routing; pending orders; brackets or OCO; automatic stop execution; automatic
take-profit lifecycle; trailing or otherwise modifiable stops; reversal;
hedging; multiple independent simultaneous positions; fractional contracts;
commission or slippage modeling; configurable fills; durable unrealized P/L or
market marks; persistence redesign; or cached aggregate persistence.
