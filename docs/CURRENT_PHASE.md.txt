## Objective

Deliver the first user-facing historical Replay surface on top of v1.6's
provider-neutral local market-data foundation: one deterministic candlestick
chart driven by a canonical UTC cursor, with no look-ahead and no coupling to
the existing journal Backtest feature.

---

## Delivered Scope

### Replay surface

- A separate **Replay** application tab.
- One Lightweight Charts 5.2.1 candlestick pane.
- Local NQ and ES contract-series selection by root and `MM-YY` expiry.
- NinjaTrader historical-file import through the Replay UI.
- Play and Pause.
- Replay speeds: **1×, 2×, 5×, 10×, 30×, 60×, and 300×**.
- Step Forward to the earliest actual 1-minute bar close after the cursor.
- Go To an exact UTC instant, including an instant inside a market gap.
- **1m, 5m, 15m, and 1h** display timeframes.
- A persistent cursor readout containing America/New_York wall time, its UTC
  offset, and the unambiguous ISO-UTC instant.

### Replay correctness

- The Replay cursor is deterministic UTC epoch time.
- Chart coordinates remain real UTC epoch seconds; timestamps are never shifted
  to imitate local time.
- America/New_York formatting distinguishes repeated DST fall-back wall times by
  offset and ISO-UTC identity.
- A canonical 1-minute bar is revealed only after its close. No partial
  1-minute candle is displayed.
- Chart autoscale, tooltips and derived timeframes receive only revealed bars;
  buffered future OHLCV cannot leak into the display.
- 5m, 15m and 1h bars fold only already-revealed canonical 1-minute bars,
  including volume, and never synthesize missing minutes.
- Real market gaps remain absent while the cursor may occupy an exact instant
  inside a gap.

### Runtime and storage boundaries

- Initial reads are windowed rather than full-history loads.
- Forward prefetch is buffer-only and does not advance cursor or authoritative
  coverage.
- Coverage advances only through successful authoritative extension.
- Cursor-driven retention bounds the in-memory history window.
- The IndexedDB historical reader prunes committed UTC days to the requested
  canonical range.
- Same-App imports enter an explicit barrier: playback pauses, the old buffer is
  hidden, stale reads cannot commit, and settlement establishes a fresh paused
  session.
- Authority tokens prevent stale asynchronous reads, prefetches, imports and
  detached owners from publishing over newer state.
- One App-lifetime Replay runtime owns the cursor and lifecycle; React
  StrictMode attach/detach remains symmetric with one animation-frame loop and
  one visibility listener.
- Page Visibility temporarily suspends active playback. Restoration re-anchors
  the monotonic clock and automatically resumes only valid prior play intent,
  excluding hidden elapsed time and respecting Pause, import, error, ended and
  detached-owner state.
- Operational storage failures surface as `read_failed`; proven absence
  surfaces as `series_unavailable`; a valid empty window remains successful
  empty data.
- The production bundle and manual Lightweight Charts chunk load correctly
  beneath the GitHub Pages base `/Trading-Journal-AI-v22/`.

## Known Limitations

Non-blocking. None is a release blocker.

1. **Cross-tab/external market-data mutation.** A running Replay session does
   not detect market-data changes made by another tab or external actor.
   Supported imports cannot contradict an existing canonical 1-minute OHLCV
   bar, but newly added missing observations can leave the running snapshot and
   derived HTF stale until reset or reload.

2. **L-5 App identity destruction.** Sign-out or user switch during a pending
   import can degrade to the accepted external snapshot-staleness class. Replay
   adds no persistence or authentication coupling to eliminate that case.

3. **Lightweight Charts UTC tick weighting.** Coordinates remain real UTC epoch
   seconds and labels are America/New_York formatted. Lightweight Charts still
   selects tick weights on UTC boundaries, which can produce a minor cosmetic
   label-placement mismatch around local date boundaries.

4. **Market gaps.** Missing minutes remain absent. Candles are ordinally
   adjacent on the Lightweight Charts time axis while the Replay cursor can sit
   inside the real missing interval.

5. **Import-status message.** The latest import success message can remain
   visible after another series is loaded. It is informational only: selected
   series, availability, Replay cursor and chart data remain correct.

All eight v1.6 historical-cache limitations remain applicable to the underlying
data foundation and are preserved in the v1.6 changelog record.
